# Security review (bounded, 2026-10-13)

A bounded security review of the tree at SHA `5aebc7c8` was done on 2026-10-13.
This page records the findings and what happened to each. It is a summary of the
reviewer's report, not the report itself, and it is not a penetration test: no
exploitation against the running lab is recorded here. Finding identifiers (A1, M1,
B3, ...) are the reviewer's. The reviewer's file and line references were not
supplied to this page; the file references below were found by reading the tree
now and may move.

## Residual risks that no row above removes

- **Delivery guarantees.** The job queue is at-least-once (G3 in [../IDEMPOTENCY.md](../IDEMPOTENCY.md)) and effects on remote Engines are at-least-once (G4): after a crash or a lost lease an Engine can see a job again. B3 cancellation is best effort, only for an Engine that declares the capability, and no engine in the tree implements `DELETE`, so today nothing is sent. Distributed exactly-once execution is not claimed and does not exist.
- **B8:**
  - In thread mode (the default, and what the lab runs) the 3 strict `xfail` limitations remain.
  - Process mode was not exercised in the lab container; it does not fit 256 MiB at the current settings.
  - A `MemoryError` while a request body is parsed now answers 500 `internal_error` in both modes; thread mode answered 400 `http_error` before (documented in `SERVICE.md`).
  - The `MemoryError` handling in `bim-core` (`compiler.py`) was not changed, to keep the digests (stated by the controller; `SERVICE.md` does not mention it, and a search of `bim-toolchain` documents and `bim-core/src` found no `MemoryError` there).
- **SPACE integration is on hold and untested:** nothing in the tests talks to SPACE or SPHERE. The anonymous privacy observation of the private SPHERE pricing ([SPHERE_PRIVACY.md](SPHERE_PRIVACY.md)) is a single observation of a single version; the by-id route is unverified, and the historical monthly counters of the private development pricing are unresolved.
- **IR order:** 64 of 82 packages have another IR from the canonical ZIP than from the directory; MAJOR decision pending.
